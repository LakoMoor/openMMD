01 / YOUR WORKSPACE

From a first pose
to the next frame.

A textured viewport, bone and morph controls, and an animation timeline. Familiar tools, with a foundation built in the open.

ACTUAL APPLICATION CAPTURES
01 / IMPORT

Bring your model.

Open PMD or the supported PMX subset with its textures. Try the procedural demo if you just want to look around.

02 / POSE

Find the expression.

Move bones, adjust facial morphs, and bring in a VPD pose. Undo and redo as you work.

03 / ANIMATE

Make it move.

Play VMD motion, edit bone and morph keys, and follow animated camera and light tracks.

02 / MORE THAN A MODEL

Give the scene
a point of view.

Import camera and light VMDs, even before loading a model. Follow the shot, play visibility and IK switches, and export scene motion separately.

PMM v1/v2 projects support a growing set of editing features, including desktop camera and light keys. Compatibility remains partial.

03 / PROJECT STATUS

Know your tools.
Know their limits.

This is an early editor. Full MMD shading, physics, accessories and complete PMM behavior are still in development.

Current file format support and limitations
Format / systemWorking todayStill ahead
PMDModelImplemented subset

Mesh, diffuse textures, bones, vertex morphs and basic IK.

Rigid-body physics, complete materials and exact MMD IK behavior.
PMX 2.0 / 2.1ModelPartial

Mesh, four bone influences, skeleton, vertex morphs and diffuse materials.

SDEF/QDEF use linear skinning. Advanced morphs and bone behavior are not evaluated; soft bodies are rejected. Details ↗
VMD / VPDMotion / poseMotion + scene tracks

Bone/morph motion, camera/light playback, visibility and IK switches. VPD import/export.

Self-shadow keys are preserved but not rendered. Camera transforms and IK are approximate. Details ↗
PMM v1 / v2ProjectPartial editing

Bone/morph tracks, camera/light playback and key editing.

Accessories, physics, shadows and full editor settings. Camera equivalence with MMD is unverified. Keep original projects.
WebGPU / WASMBrowserWorking browser build

Local files, shared rendering and animation, key editing and exports.

A WebGPU-capable browser is required. Advanced curves and PMM camera/light key editing remain desktop-only. Mobile GPU support depends on the browser and device.
macOS / Windows / LinuxDesktopCI on all three

Builds and ordinary tests run on all three platforms.

Desktop GPU playback is currently verified on macOS. Windows and Linux runtime testing remains ahead.
